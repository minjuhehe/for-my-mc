Before you join

What you need to play

No mods or downloads from us. A normal Minecraft Java account is enough.

  1. Own Minecraft: Java EditionFor PC, Mac or Linux. Bedrock (phone, console, Windows store "Bedrock") can't join yet.
  2. Launch version 1.21In the launcher, pick the latest release. It should say 1.21.x.
  3. Get on the whitelistSend your exact Minecraft username to an admin. We add you before you can join.
  4. Add the serverMultiplayer → Add Server → paste play.lostsky.example → Done.
  5. Type /isYou get your own island. Then check /chapter to see where the story is.

How it works

Your island. Everyone's Citadel.

/is

Grow your island

Start on a tiny floating island. Farm, build generators, and play with up to three friends.

/ruin

Explore the ruins

Every two hours a ruin drifts in for 45 minutes. It has loot, mobs, puzzles and relics.

/donate

Return the relics

Give relics to the Sky Citadel. You earn points and money, and the whole server moves the story forward.

The story

Five chapters to the Citadel

Each chapter unlocks for everyone when the server's donated relic points reach its goal.

Rules

Keep the sky friendly

  • No griefing or stealingIslands are protected. Block logs are kept, so we can check anything.
  • Share the ruinsDon't block entrances, trap other players or break puzzles.
  • No cheats or exploitsFound a bug or a dupe? Report it to an admin and get a reward.
  • No PvPPvP is off everywhere, ruins included.
  • Be kind in chatNo hate, spam or advertising.
  • One account eachNo alt accounts farming relics.
